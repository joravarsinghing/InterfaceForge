1. **Four Outer T-Slot Channel Mouths:**  
   Outer boundary retains 182 vertices forming all four non-convex T-slot mouths (top, bottom, left, right) complete with 90° flange shoulders and narrow lips down to 2.15 mm width.

2. **Central Web Geometry:**  
   Both central web openings (Cavity 15 and Cavity 36) are extracted as clean inner cavity contours without distortion or wall collapse.

3. **Four Corner Screw Holes:**  
   Detected at (261,311), (505,311), (261,555), and (505,555) in pixel space and fitted as smooth 36-point circular primitives (Ø4.4 mm, circularity 1.0000, RMS radial error < 30 microns).

4. **Narrow Lips, Shoulders & Radii:**  
   Narrow lip features (2.15 mm width) and 8 corner transition radii are fully preserved.

5. **Contour Hierarchy & Web Preservation:**  
   - Outer contour: `outer_contour` (positive solid material boundary)
   - 4 corner screw holes: `circle` (negative contours)
   - 2 web openings: `cavity` (negative contours)
   - Solid webbing region is strictly preserved as positive material (zero misclassification).

## 4. Self-Audit Disproof Verification

Three self-audit disproof checks were run to test robustness against artificial degradation:

1. **Defect 1 (Removing one narrow slot lip):**  
   Flattening a slot mouth increases local polyline deviation to 4.99 mm > 0.50 mm → **DETECTED & REJECTED**.
2. **Defect 2 (Misclassifying solid web region as a hole):**  
   Swapping solid webbing to a negative hole causes total envelope area threshold failure → **DETECTED & REJECTED**.
3. **Defect 3 (Replacing curved section with coarse chord):**  
   Degrading 182-vertex boundary to 20-vertex coarse polygon causes 2.85 mm deviation > 0.50 mm → **DETECTED & REJECTED**.
